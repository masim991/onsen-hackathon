Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

There is no sign-up or login, and we do not collect personal information such as names or email addresses. We do not store visitor records on a server.

Stored in your browser

Your favorites, light/dark mode and whether you have seen the opening screen are stored only in your own browser. Clearing the browser’s site data removes them.

Third-party connections

To show the page, your browser connects directly to Supabase (listings), Wikimedia Commons (photos), Google Fonts and jsDelivr (fonts), ExchangeRate-API and Frankfurter (exchange rates) and the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (maps). Your IP address and similar connection data may be sent to those services, which follow their own policies.

Hosting

This site is served through Vercel, and the hosting provider may keep access logs for a period of time.
