Planning an onsen trip

Before booking a ryokan

Conditions vary by property and plan. Check the booking page and the property’s own guidance.

Rooms and bedding
Confirm whether the room has futons or beds, enough bedding for your party, and smoking restrictions.
Baths
Check whether a room bath uses hot spring water, bathing hours, private bath reservations and extra charges.
Accessibility and guest policies
Ask about stairs, lifts, children and tattoo policies where relevant.
Meals and arrival
Check which meals are included, dietary requests, meal location and the latest arrival time for dinner.
Total and cancellation
Confirm whether the rate is per person or per room, taxes including bathing tax, extra charges, payment and refund terms.
Transport
Check check-in times, shuttle reservations and the last public transport service.
Sources

Japan National Tourism Organization: ryokan guide. Confirm property-specific conditions directly.

Before visiting an onsen

Day-use access and hours
Many ryokan do not accept day visitors or admit them only at certain times. Check the last admission and closure days.
Prices and payment
Rates can vary by day and may exclude bathing tax. Some facilities accept cash only.
Bath arrangements
Check whether baths are separate, mixed, rotated by time or reserved for women at certain times.
Towels and amenities
Towels may be unavailable or cost extra for day visitors.
Private or in-room baths
Check advance booking, fees and whether the water is hot spring water.
Water and treatment
Look at water type and whether it is heated, diluted or recirculated. Source temperature is different from bath temperature.
Getting there
Check bus schedules, shuttles, parking and winter road conditions.
